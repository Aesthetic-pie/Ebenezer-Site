About

Nestled beautifully in the tranquil surroundings of QwaQwa, Ebenezer Lodge and Conference is more than just a place to stay, it is a unique gem designed for you to pause, breathe, and feel at home.

Created for travelers, families, couples, professionals and conference guests seeking both comfort and serenity, our lodge combines warm hospitality with peaceful surroundings that invite true relaxation. From the moment you arrive, you are welcomed by a calm atmosphere, beautiful garden spaces, and thoughtfully prepared accomodation designed to make every stay restful and memorable.

Wake up to fresh mountain air, sit down to our quiet farmhouse-style morning setups, and immerse yourself in real regional hospitality.

Nearby Attractions

QwaQwa sits at the foot of the Maluti Mountains, so there's plenty to explore within a short drive of the lodge.

Witsieshoek Mountain

Wake up to a breathtaking view of the Witsieshoek mountain to refresh the soul and embrace the beauty of nature.

Basotho Cultural Village

A living showcase of traditional Basotho architecture, customs and crafts.

Golden Gate Highlands National Park

Dramatic sandstone cliffs and rolling grasslands along the Maluti Mountains.

Sentinel Peak Trail

A well-loved Drakensberg hiking route with views out toward Tugela Falls.

Kgodumodumo Dinosaur Interpretation Centre

A state-of-the-art interactive facility that showcases world-class archaeology.
